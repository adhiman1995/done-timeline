## Full description (max 4000)

```
Ticked is a calm, simple place to record what you've finished, and to see it all on a timeline.

To-do apps are about what's next. Ticked is about what you've done. Log a task the moment you finish it and watch your days fill up: work you shipped, workouts you did, chapters you read, errands you finally ran.

WHAT YOU GET
• A clean timeline of everything you've completed, grouped by day
• Categories with colours, so your work, health, learning and personal wins are easy to tell apart
• Stars for the moments worth remembering
• Your streak, best day and all-time count at a glance
• Filters for today, the last 7 or 30 days, or all time, plus search

WORKS EVERYWHERE
• Works offline. Everything is saved on your phone first and syncs when you're back online
• Syncs with the Ticked website, so you can log from your computer too
• Light and dark themes that follow your phone

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Sign in with your Google account. No passwords to remember
• Your tasks are visible only to you
• No ads, no tracking, no selling your data
• Delete your account and all your data at any time, right in the app
```

## App content: answers for Play Console

**Privacy policy**: https://adhiman1995.github.io/done-timeline/privacy.html

**Ads**: No, the app doesn't contain ads.

**App access**: All functionality requires signing in with Google. Choose "All or some functionality is restricted" and add instructions for reviewers:
> Tap "Continue with Google" and sign in with any Google account. Every account gets its own empty timeline; tap "Add completed task" to add one. No special credentials are needed.

**Content rating questionnaire**: category "Utility, productivity, communication or other". Answer **No** to everything: no violence, sexual content, profanity, drugs, gambling or user-to-user communication (tasks are private, never shared). Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3.

**Target audience**: 18 and over (or 13+). Not designed for children.

**News app**: No. **COVID-19 contact tracing**: No. **Government app**: No. **Financial features**: None. **Health apps**: None.

### Data safety

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **Yes** |
| Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? | **Yes** |
| Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? | **Yes** (in app, on the website, and by email) |

Data types **collected** (none are **shared** with third parties):

| Data type | Collected | Optional? | Purposes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal info → **Name** | Yes | Required | App functionality, Account management |
| Personal info → **Email address** | Yes | Required | App functionality, Account management |
| Personal info → **User IDs** | Yes | Required | App functionality, Account management |
| Photos and videos → **Photos** | No. Only the Google profile-photo *link* is received; it is not stored by Ticked. | n/a | n/a |
| App activity → **Other user-generated content** (task titles, notes, categories) | Yes | Required | App functionality |

For each collected type: processed ephemerally? **No**. Data is stored to sync between devices.

Not collected: location, financial info, health and fitness, messages, contacts, calendar, files, audio, web browsing, app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics, device IDs.

Firebase (Google) acts as a *service provider* processing data on our behalf, so that is **not** "sharing" under Play's definitions.
