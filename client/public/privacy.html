Privacy Policy

Ticked · Last updated 21 November 2026

Summary

Ticked lets you log tasks you've completed and see them on a timeline, on Android and on the web. We collect only what's needed to sign you in and keep your tasks in sync. We don't show ads, we don't use analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics: name, email address, profile picture link and a unique account ID, provided by Google when you choose "Continue with Google".
  • The tasks you enter: title, notes, category, whether it's starred, and when it was completed, created and last changed.

Ticked doesn't access your contacts, location, files, camera, microphone or other apps.

How we use it

Only to sign you in, store your tasks, and sync them between your devices and the Ticked website. Your tasks are private to your account: no other user can see them.

Where it's stored

Your account and tasks are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which Ticked uses as its service provider. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by access rules that only let your signed-in account read or change your tasks. The Android app also keeps a copy on your phone so it works offline.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone. It's processed only by Google Firebase to run the service, or disclosed if required by law.

Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your Ticked account and all your tasks at any time:

  • In the Android app: tap the account icon (top right) → Delete account.
  • On the website: sign in at the Ticked website, click your avatar → Delete account.
  • By email: write to alokdhiman018@gmail.com from the Google account you use with Ticked, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Deletion removes your account and every task from our servers right away. Nothing is kept afterwards. Copies on a phone that is offline are removed when it next connects or when the app is uninstalled.

Keeping your data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual tasks at any time, and deleting your account removes everything.

Children

Ticked isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions? Email alokdhiman018@gmail.com.
